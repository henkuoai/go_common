# Short-form video script · YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels

**Length target:** 30–40 seconds.
**Aspect:** 9:16 vertical.
**Link:** `example.com/webp-to-png/`
**Format:** screen recording. The size number jumping is the visual hook — build the whole video around it.

---

## Script

**[0:00–0:03] HOOK**

> "This image is 295 kilobytes. Watch what happens when I turn it into a PNG."

*Visual: hold up the file in a folder view, size column visible. Then a hard cut.*

---

**[0:03–0:07] THE REVEAL**

> "2.4 megabytes. **Eight times bigger.** And no, the tool isn't broken."

*Visual: the size column after conversion, the number visibly larger. Zoom in on it.*

---

**[0:07–0:14] WHY**

> "PNG is lossless. That WebP you downloaded was not — it got small by throwing information away. PNG refuses to do that. It puts everything back. That's the trade."

*Visual: side-by-side of the two file sizes with the % difference overlaid in large type.*

---

**[0:14–0:20] THE PART THAT BREAKS PEOPLE'S BRAINS**

> "Here's the weird part. The *smaller* your original file, the worse the blow-up. A 27 kilobyte image went to 63 kilobytes. A 29 kilobyte one went to 361."

*Visual: quick three-row table animating in.*

---

**[0:20–0:27] THE TRAP**

> "And if you're dragging the quality slider on a PNG — that does nothing. PNG has no quality setting. It's lossless. The *only* thing that makes a PNG smaller is fewer pixels."

*Visual: drag the quality slider all the way left and right while the file size stays frozen. Then change the resize dropdown and the size visibly drops.*

---

**[0:27–0:34] WHEN YOU ACTUALLY NEED PNG**

> "So when *should* you use PNG? Only one real reason: transparency. PNG keeps it, JPEG doesn't. If you don't need transparency, go to JPEG instead — this same image came out 39% *smaller* than the original."

*Visual: a transparent logo on a checkerboard → PNG keeps the checkerboard → JPEG fills it in solid white. This is the shot that gets screenshotted.*

---

**[0:34–0:38] CTA**

> "Converter's free, runs in your browser, nothing gets uploaded. Link's in the description."

---

## Production notes

- **The frozen quality slider is the best 3 seconds in this video.** Actually drag it and show the size not moving. Nobody expects a control that does nothing, and it's the moment people decide you know what you're talking about.
- **The transparency fill-in shot is the second best.** Checkerboard → white background. If you can, show it *after* the user has already downloaded, so the lesson lands as "this could have happened to you."
- These are two separate videos if you want them: one on "PNG is huge," one on "transparency silently disappears." Split them if the combined version feels rushed.
- Caption every frame — Shorts get watched on mute, and the numbers are the shareable part.
