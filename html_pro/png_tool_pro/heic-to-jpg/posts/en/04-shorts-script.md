# Short-form video script · YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels

**Length target:** 25–35 seconds. Anything past 40 loses retention on this topic.
**Aspect:** 9:16 vertical.
**Link:** `example.com/heic-to-jpg/`
**Posted as:** screen recording, no face required. Record the drop zone and the result row — the speed of the conversion is itself the hook.

---

## Script

**[0:00–0:03] HOOK — say exactly this, no wind-up**

> "Your iPhone photos won't open on Windows, and renaming them to .jpg makes it worse."

*Visual: blank thumbnail tile in File Explorer next to a crisp photo on a phone. Split screen.*

---

**[0:03–0:09] THE PROBLEM**

> "Your phone doesn't shoot JPEG anymore. It shoots HEIC. Half the file size, same picture — which is great, until you try to open it anywhere that isn't an iPhone."

*Visual: cursor double-clicks the file. Error dialog. Cut.*

---

**[0:09–0:14] KILL THE BAD ADVICE**

> "Renaming it does nothing. It's still HEIC inside. You'll just get a corrupt file error and think your photos are gone. They're not."

*Visual: rename the file on screen → open → error. Big red X overlay.*

---

**[0:14–0:24] THE FIX**

> "Two ways. One: on your iPhone, Settings, Camera, Formats, switch to Most Compatible. That fixes every photo you take from now on — not the ones you already have."
>
> "Two: convert them. And watch this — I'm going to turn off my wifi first."

*Visual: toggle wifi off in the menu bar. Then drop 5 HEIC files into a browser page. Rows fill in, thumbnails appear, sizes show. This is the money shot — it lands the "no upload needed" point visually instead of claiming it.*

---

**[0:24–0:31] THE PAYOFF + THE SURPRISE**

> "Five photos, no internet, nothing uploaded anywhere. And here's the part nobody tells you: the JPG comes out **bigger**. Mine went up 68%. That's not a bug — HEIC is just a more efficient format. So if size matters, drop the quality slider."

*Visual: the size column showing +68% in orange. Then drag the quality slider and watch it drop.*

---

**[0:31–0:34] CTA**

> "Link's in the description. It's free and there's no watermark."

## Production notes

- **The wifi-off moment is the whole video.** If you only get one shot right, get that one. It converts an unverifiable privacy claim into something the viewer watches happen.
- Don't say "our tool" / "my tool" in the first 20 seconds. Lead with the fix; the product arrives as the answer, not the pitch.
- Captions on screen for the full runtime — a large share of Shorts get watched on mute, and the numbers (+68%, 5 photos) are the part people screenshot.
- Pin a comment with the link. Description links are frequently not clickable on Shorts.
