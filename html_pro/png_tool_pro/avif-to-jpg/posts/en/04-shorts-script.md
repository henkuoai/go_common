# Short-form video script · YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels

**Length target:** 25–35 seconds.
**Aspect:** 9:16 vertical.
**Link:** `example.com/avif-to-jpg/`
**Format:** screen recording. The hook is "newest format, oldest problem."

---

## Script

**[0:00–0:03] HOOK**

> "This image file is brand new technology. Nothing on your computer can open it. Let me show you why."

*Visual: a folder with an .avif file. Double-click → nothing → unsupported error. Hold on the error.*

---

**[0:03–0:10] WHAT IT IS**

> "It's called AVIF, and it's built on AV1 — the same video codec Netflix uses to stream. That's the point: it gets a photo into about **half the size of a JPEG**. Sites love it because bandwidth costs money."

*Visual: split screen — a JPEG and an AVIF of the same image side by side, file sizes labelled. Big "50% smaller."*

---

**[0:10–0:16] WHY IT BREAKS**

> "Your browser opens it fine. Your Photoshop doesn't. Your Word doesn't. Basically everything that isn't a browser says no."

*Visual: try to open in PS → error. Try to insert in Word → greyed out. Quick cuts.*

---

**[0:16–0:21] THE TRAP**

> "And whatever you do — do **not** just rename it to dot-jpg. It's still AVIF inside. You'll get a corrupt file error and think your download was bad. It wasn't."

*Visual: rename on screen → open → error dialog. Big red X.*

---

**[0:21–0:30] THE SURPRISE**

> "Now here's the part that gets everybody. When you convert it to JPG, the file gets **bigger**. Mine went up 163% at high quality. That's not a bad converter — AVIF is just more efficient, so a faithful JPEG costs more bytes. Set the quality to about 70 and it breaks even."

*Visual: the quality slider sweeping while the size number updates live. Around q70 the "vs original" figure crosses zero and turns green. This is the shot.*

---

**[0:30–0:34] CTA**

> "Converter runs in your browser, nothing gets uploaded, and it's free. Link's in the description."

## Production notes

- **The slider-sweep at the end is the entire video.** Watching the size figure cross from "+163%" to "−6%" as you drag teaches the whole concept in three seconds without a single sentence of explanation. Record it slowly and make sure both numbers are legible.
- Keep the "don't rename it" beat — it's short, it's actionable, and it's the mistake most viewers have already made.
- This audience skews technical (webdev / photography). You can use "AV1" and "codec" without defining them, which makes the video feel like it's for them rather than for everyone.
- Caption everything. Assume mute.
